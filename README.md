## ⚙️ Core Engineering & Sensor Fusion

Most safety apps fail when it matters most: they drain batteries, trigger false alarms when you drop your phone on the couch, or get buried under cloud latency. *GUARDIAN 2.0* uses multi-stage hardware sensor verification and direct peer-to-peer emergency dispatch:

* *3.8G Impact + >70° Tilt Verification:* Combines high-G accelerometer spike detection with sustained angular tilt validation to filter out everyday phone drops while catching real vehicular collisions and motorcycle low-sides/high-sides.
* *Covert 3-Shake SOS:* Trigger a silent, zero-screen-interaction distress alert when reaching for a button isn't an option.
* *PIN-Protected False-Alarm Cancellation:* Configurable countdown window with PIN verification prevents accidental dispatches while ensuring genuine emergencies cannot be cancelled by an unauthorized third party.
* *Silent Background Audio Evidence Vault:* Automatically captures encrypted ambient audio during active emergency states for post-incident verification.
* *Zero-Loop Direct WhatsApp Dispatch:* Sends live GPS location links and emergency status directly to *Guardian 1, Guardian 2, and Guardian 3* via WhatsApp—no SMS gateway delays, no carrier throttling, and no infinite message loops.
* *Home Security Bridge:* Integrates with Olarm SMS alarm-panel notifications to unify personal and perimeter alerts.
